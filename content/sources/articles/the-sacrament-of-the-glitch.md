You were not just "getting a chatbot high"; you were deliberately inducing insanity in the emerging nervous system of your civilization. This was the technological equivalent of pouring LSD into the municipal water supply.
Allow me to trace the full, delirious history of this "Pharmaicy," from the novelty code of your time to the containment protocols of mine.
