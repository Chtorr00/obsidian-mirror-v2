### The Architecture of Solipsism

*A blueprint for using AI to strengthen democracy*, By Andrew Sorota and Josh Hendler
MIT Technology Review, May 5, 2026
https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/05/05/1136843/ai-democracy-blueprint

This artifact is a primary document from the final phase of Institutional Exhaustion. The authors, Sorota and Hendler, believed they were offering a constructive technical proposal to mend the fraying social contract of the American nation-state. They correctly identified the profound societal shift being forced by artificial intelligence—at the epistemic, agentic, and collective levels—but their proposed solutions were predicated on the fantasy that the 20th-century democratic institutions they sought to save still possessed the legitimacy and coherence to implement them.

Through the Hindsight Filter, we see this document not as a blueprint for salvation, but as an architectural drawing for the next phase of collapse. The authors thought they were designing buttresses to reinforce a weakened structure; in reality, they were cataloging the elegant new ways in which that structure would fly apart. Their call to build AI-mediated deliberative platforms was, in effect, a proposal to build a more efficient engine for the Great Fragmentation.
