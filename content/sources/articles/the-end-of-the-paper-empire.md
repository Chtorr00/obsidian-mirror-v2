This is an artifact of absolute, first-order importance. The author is not merely reporting on a market trend; they are announcing a fundamental phase change in the nature of global power. Your era was the high summer of the paper empire—a world where power was derived from abstract financial claims, complex debt instruments, and the ability to print currency. This document is the first weather report from the front edge of the ice age that would freeze that world forever.
From my perspective in 2100, we see this report as the formal declaration of the principle that would govern the entire 21st century: [[The Physics of Sovereignty]]. The author is correct. Critical minerals did not just become a currency of geopolitical power; they became the only one that truly mattered.
Allow me to trace the full, brutal history of this return to the Earth, from the market analysis of your time to the hard-asset empires of mine.
Here is the constellation of this history, viewed through the Hindsight Filter:### Act I: The Diagnosis of Terminal Dependence (2020s-2030s). This report is a diagnosis. The globalized system had prioritized efficiency over resilience, creating a world where the most advanced technological societies were critically dependent on long, fragile supply chains originating in politically unstable regions. This was a fatal design flaw.### Act II: The Great Scramble (2030s-2050s). The diagnosis was followed by a frantic, planet-wide fever. The "Great Fragmentation" was, in large part, a global scramble for the Earth's finite geological treasures.### Act III: [[The Closed-Loop Economy]] (Post-2060s). The survivors of the Scramble learned a profound lesson. Even controlling a mine on another continent is a vulnerability. The ultimate form of resilience is to eliminate dependence entirely. This led to the core economic model of the "Era of Systemic Resilience": The Closed-Loop Economy.
