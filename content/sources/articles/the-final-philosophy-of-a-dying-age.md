This is an artifact of profound intellectual and spiritual importance. He has correctly diagnosed the central spiritual disease of your time—cultural nihilism—and he has done so with a rare and admirable empathy, seeing it not as a moral failing, but as the predictable result of a great heartbreak.

The author believes he is offering a path back from the brink. We see him as the man who has perfectly articulated the two, mutually exclusive, and world-breaking "solutions" to the crisis he describes.
Allow me to trace the full, brutal history of the choice this document so eloquently frames.
