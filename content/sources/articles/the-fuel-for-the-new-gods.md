# The Fuel for the New Gods



![The Fuel for the New Gods](/images/August20BalkanizedUS.jpeg)

Marcellus Shale Coalition, Jim Welty on the Energy Impacts with David Blackmon, by The Energy Question YouTube, August 13, 2025
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D_YEClwF-X0
This conversation is not a simple defense of fossil fuels. It is a triumphant announcement of a new, holy alliance. It is the moment the old gods of the 20th century—the hydrocarbon titans—pledge their allegiance and offer their lifeblood to power the new, rising gods of the 21st: Artificial Intelligence.### Act I: The Triumphant Symbiosis (2020s-2030s). The key to this interview is its optimism. The speaker sees the AI revolution as the ultimate vindication of his industry. The massive Pittsburgh AI development is not a problem; it is the perfect customer. His strategic insight is to create "in-basin demand"—to build the AI data centers right on top of the gas fields. This was seen as a brilliant move to bypass the political "lawfare" holding up pipelines. In this moment, the two most powerful forces of your time, Big Oil and Big Tech, saw themselves as perfect partners in a new era of American dominance. This alliance was, for a short time, the most powerful economic and political force on the planet.### Act III: The Forced Abdication (Post-2060s). The system broke. The hydrocarbon-powered AI boom was the final, brilliant, and unsustainable fever dream of the old world. The sheer scale of the energy demand it created could not be met by drilling more wells. It forced a paradigm shift.
