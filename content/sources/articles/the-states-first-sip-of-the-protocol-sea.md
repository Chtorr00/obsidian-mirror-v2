### The State's First Sip of the Protocol Sea

**Citation:** *Gooden Reveals Historic U.S. Military Use of Bitcoin Node*, By Congressman Lance Gooden, US House website, April 22, 2026. https://gooden.house.gov/press-releases?ID=232F2920-4821-4B84-A676-A9521924FEBC

**The Summary:** This artifact documents a critical inflection point in the Age of Institutional Exhaustion: the moment a 20th-century sovereign power first attempted to formally integrate a decentralized, non-sovereign protocol into its national security apparatus. The exchange between Congressman Gooden and Admiral Paparo is revealing. They believed they were discussing the acquisition of a new "computer science tool"—a novel method of cryptography to "secure networks and project power." They frame the Bitcoin protocol as an instrument, an inert object to be wielded in geopolitical competition, akin to a new cryptographic standard or another strategic resource like oil.

Through the Hindsight Filter, we see their profound conceptual error. They were not acquiring a tool; they were interfacing with an alternative logic of power. They thought they could use the protocol’s properties—immutable time-stamping, decentralized verification—to reinforce the centralized nation-state. What they were actually documenting was the state’s first, tentative step toward sanctioning its own eventual irrelevance in matters of trust and settlement.
