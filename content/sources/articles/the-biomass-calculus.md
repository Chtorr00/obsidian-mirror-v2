### The Biomass Calculus

*System of A Downs*, By Librarian of Celaeno
Librarian of Calaeno Substack, June 11, 2026
https://substack.com/home/post/p-201286301

This document captures a critical inflection point in the moral architecture of the late American empire. The author believed he was engaging in a spiritual defense of the intellectually disabled against a resurgent, utilitarian paganism taking root within right-wing political discourse. He astutely noticed the creeping logic that reclassified human beings from natural "dependents" to disposable "burdens," correctly warning his audience that the managerial state would eventually apply this exact optimization calculus to the average citizen. 

Applying the Hindsight Filter, we observe the author diagnosing a terminal symptom while misapprehending the disease. He framed this shift toward eugenic utilitarianism as a spiritual failing—a choice of hedonism over Christian sacrifice. In truth, he was documenting the psychological precursor to the triage protocols of the 2030s. The "hedonism" he critiqued was actually the desperate thrashing of a society attempting to optimize its dwindling resources. The author thought he was fighting a theological battle for the soul of his political faction; we know he was transcribing the ideological justification for the brutal bio-political contractions that were about to follow.### Act I: The Triage Ideology (2020s-2030s)

* **Institutional Exhaustion:** The sudden, vicious debate over who constituted a "burden" arose precisely because the surplus mechanisms of the 20th-century welfare state were failing. Institutional Exhaustion meant the state could no longer seamlessly subsidize infinite dependents, prompting both citizens and the managerial class to quietly renegotiate the value of human life based on economic output, masking their material panic behind the rhetoric of autonomy and optimization.
* **The Great Culling:** The author's warning that the elite would eventually view the average citizen as "expendable biomass" was a direct premonition of The Great Culling. The casual social media discourse of the 2020s that justified the termination of the sub-optimal served to normalize the precise algorithms the state would later use to allocate rationing, healthcare, and security during the severe resource shocks of the subsequent decade.### Act II: The Biopolitical Contraction (2030s-2050s)

* **Decentralized Resilient Enclaves:** The "heroic sacrifice" and love-based community the author prescribed did not save the macro-state, but it became the exact survival mechanism for Decentralized Resilient Enclaves. As central infrastructure failed, physical survival depended entirely on the willingness of localized groups to absorb burdens and sacrifice for one another. Those enclaves that adopted the cold utilitarianism of the 2020s fractured under pressure, while those bound by mutual, sacrificial obligation endured.
* **Collapse Of Adjudication:** The complete breakdown of a shared moral framework regarding the sanctity of life led directly to the Collapse Of Adjudication. Without a universal consensus on who deserved protection, federal legal standards dissolved. Different regions adopted radically different bio-ethical baselines, leading to a patchwork of territories where the definition of a "person" versus a "burden" dictated whether one received sanctuary or expulsion.### Act III: The Sacrificial Reconstitution (Post-2060s)

* **Physical World:** The transhumanist and eugenic desires to engineer away all physical and mental defects were rejected by the bioconservative stacks, who returned to the Physical World and accepted biological limitation as intrinsic to the human condition. While the transhuman factions continued to pursue active genetic optimization in their enclaves, the bioconservative majority established a societal recodification where worth is no longer measured by frictionless utility, but by one's embeddedness in a tangible, local network of care.
* **Pragmatic Reformation:** The spiritual sickness the author diagnosed was eventually cured not by a top-down religious revival, but by the Pragmatic Reformation. The harsh decades of the mid-century proved empirically that hyper-individualist societies simply do not survive kinetic shocks. The ethic of sacrifice became the dominant paradigm because history demonstrated it was the only mathematically viable strategy for long-term species survival.

They sought to mathematically optimize the human condition, failing to realize that a society which eradicates its burdens also eradicates the very friction that binds it together.
