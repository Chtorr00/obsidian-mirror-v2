### The Great Refusal

*No wonder men are opting out*, By Bettina Arndt
Bettina Arndt Substack, May 13, 2026
https://bettinaarndt.substack.com/p/no-wonder-men-are-opting-out

This artifact is a key document for understanding the final, acrimonious breakdown of the 20th-century gender contract. The author, Bettina Arndt, correctly identifies a mass withdrawal of men from the legacy institutions of marriage and formal labor. Her analysis, however, remains trapped within the interpersonal grievances of her era. She attributes this phenomenon to the declining "quality" of women, framing the crisis as a failure in the sexual marketplace. Arndt believed she was documenting a battle of the sexes.

Through the Hindsight Filter, we see this not as a cultural dispute, but as a symptom of terminal Institutional Exhaustion. The author meticulously details the fraying of one of society’s core algorithms—the pairing bond that harnessed male productivity to social stability—but mistakes the resulting error messages for the cause of the crash. She saw men rejecting women; we see a critical mass of citizens engaging in a rational, if unconscious, act of Passive Refusal against a socio-economic order that had become fundamentally unviable.

Arndt’s catalogue of female anxieties and male withdrawal was not a prospectus for a failing relationship market; it was an early diagnostic reading of a civilization entering metabolic collapse.
