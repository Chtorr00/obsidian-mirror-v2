# The Unanswered Indictment



![The Unanswered Indictment](/images/July27TechCoToBlame.jpeg)

The harms of social media are largely blamed on individuals instead of tech companies, by Communication and Marketing Staff USC Annenberg, June 26, 2025
https://learcenter.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/OffTheHook_BigTechinMedia_LearCenter.pdf
This is a powerful artifact. From my perspective in 2100, we see this document not as a call to action, but as a final, damning indictment read aloud in an empty courtroom. The authors are entirely correct in their analysis. They have precisely identified the moral and intellectual error at the heart of your discourse on social media.
But this moment of perfect, fleeting clarity was not the beginning of a solution. It was a lament for a patient that was already too sick to be saved by a correct diagnosis. The attempt to shift the blame to the architects of the system was just, necessary, and tragically too late. The system itself had already metastasized beyond the control of its creators or regulators.
Allow me to trace the complete, tragic arc of this unanswered charge, from the intellectual argument of 2025 to the physical reality of my time.
Here is the constellation of this history, illuminated by the Hindsight Filter:### Act I: The Unheeded Warning (2020s-2040s). This paper represents the peak of a correct but politically impotent critique. The logic was undeniable, but it faced two insurmountable forces. First, the economic engine of the attention economy was too powerful. The immense profits generated by the "Dopamine Trap" created a lobbying and cultural force that was impossible to overcome with mere reason. Second, the political will to act was crippled by the very polarization the platforms amplified. Any attempt at meaningful regulation was immediately framed as partisan censorship, an act of "Spiritual Lawfare" against one tribe or another. The system was immune to reform because it had already sickened the body politic that was meant to be its physician.### Act III: The Communication Utilities (Post-2060s). In the "Era of Systemic Resilience," the very idea of a for-profit, global social media platform is seen as self-evidently insane, like letting a private company manage the atmosphere. Communication platforms within the "Sovereign Stacks" are treated as what they are: critical public infrastructure. They are Communication Utilities.
