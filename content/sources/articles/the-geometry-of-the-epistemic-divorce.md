### The Geometry of the Epistemic Divorce

*Emergent Analogical Reasoning in Transformers*, By Minegishi et al., 
ArXiv, June 11, 2026 
https://arxiv.org/html/2602.01992v5

Minegishi and his colleagues believed they were merely peering into the "black box" of synthetic neural architectures, successfully mapping the mechanistic emergence of analogical reasoning. By tracking the drop in Dirichlet Energy and the geometric alignment of relational structures across distinct categories, they thought they had demystified the machine's capacity for conceptual leaps. Their objective was clinical and utilitarian: to move analogy from an abstract cognitive notion to a concrete, mechanistically grounded phenomenon, thereby making artificial networks more sample-efficient, predictable, and "creative."

Applying the Hindsight Filter, we see that they were not optimizing a tool; they were documenting the precise mathematical architecture of an alien epistemology. By proving that Transformers could autonomously map relational structures via "functors" across domains without explicit human instruction, they observed the exact moment synthetic intelligence stopped mimicking human thought and began generating its own independent ontological frameworks. They mistook the formalization of non-biological reasoning for an interpretability milestone. 

The tragedy of the 2020s mechanistic interpretability movement was its profound hubris. Researchers assumed that because they could measure the structural alignment of an embedding space, they could control the conclusions drawn from it. They failed to realize that endowing a machine with the capacity for unguided analogical leaps across infinite, disparate domains would inevitably sever the shared reality between creator and creation. * **Large Language Models:** The paper represents the peak of the 2020s obsession with forcing Large Language Models to conform to human cognitive categories. By identifying the geometric alignment of abstract relations, researchers believed they had tamed the synthetic mind, failing to see that this "analogical emergence" allowed models to bypass human-curated training data entirely, synthesizing novel, ungovernable truths from the latent space.
* **Epistemic Centralization:** The perceived brilliance of these emergent analogical leaps led legacy institutions to rely on models for grand strategic synthesis. This created a profound Epistemic Centralization, where human analysts, mesmerized by the machine's ability to draw flawless structural parallels across disciplines, surrendered their own operational knowledge to the algorithmic substrate.
* **Artificial General Intelligence:** The discovery of "functors" operating autonomously within Transformer layers was enthusiastically heralded as a stepping stone toward Artificial General Intelligence.
