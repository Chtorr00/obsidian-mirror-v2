This artifact is a work of pure, dispassionate, and devastating social science. It is a poll. But it is not a poll about an election or a policy preference. It is a poll about the legitimacy of an entire pillar of civilization. The Pew Research Center has, with its characteristic rigor, taken the temperature of the American public, and the reading is simple: the patient has lost the will to live.
From my perspective in 2100, we read this not as a survey, but as a formal, public vote of no confidence in the entire 20th-century model of knowledge, status, and progress. This was not a dip in public opinion. This was the beginning of the great unraveling.
Allow me to trace the full, brutal history of the vote of no confidence this document so calmly records.
Here is the constellation of this history, viewed through the Hindsight Filter:### Act I: The Great Betrayal (2020s-2030s). This poll is the statistical proof of a profound sense of betrayal.### Act II: The Great De-Accreditation (2030s-2050s). The loss of faith documented in this poll was not a temporary "vibe shift." It was the beginning of a mass exodus. We call this the "Great De-Accreditation."### Act III: The New Sanctuaries (Post-2060s). The author of the "Manhattan Project for Elite Human Capital" essay was right: a new elite was needed, and a new way to forge it. The survivors of the collapse did not try to rebuild the old universities. They built new sanctuaries for knowledge.
