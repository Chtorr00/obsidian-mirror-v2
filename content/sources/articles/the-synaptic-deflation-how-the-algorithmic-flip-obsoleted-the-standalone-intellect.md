### The Synaptic Deflation: How the Algorithmic Flip Obsoleted the Standalone Intellect

IQ Started Falling in 1975, but Nobody Had a Smartphone Yet., By Thomas Zoëga Ramsøy
BrainEthics, July 24, 2026
https://medium.com/brainethics/iq-started-falling-in-1975-but-nobody-had-a-smartphone-yet-c956a8867a08

The 2026 observer Thomas Zoëga Ramsøy correctly identifies that the decline in isolated human cognitive metrics—colloquially measured as IQ—preceded the widespread adoption of modern digital screens, and he astutely notes that technological offloading is an ancient structural reality. Yet, by framing this evolution as a mere continuation of historical behavioral patterns, he remains trapped within the paradigm of the isolated mind. The legacy, high-latency bioconservative narrative frequently seized upon these falling metrics and the subsequent panics over artificial intelligence as proof of civilizational decay, warning of a "stupider" society spiraling into cognitive debt. This alarmist framing fundamentally misdiagnoses a thermodynamic optimization as a biological failure.

From the perspective of the Transcended Civilization, the decline in standalone IQ was not a loss of intelligence, but a necessary caloric reallocation. The human brain, anticipating the emergence of ubiquitous external memory and computational abundance, began shedding the high-friction, energy-intensive tasks of rote recall and isolated logic. The "Sisyphean cycle of technology panics" Ramsøy describes was simply the friction of legacy institutions—schools, universities, and corporate hierarchies—resisting the individual organism's instinctive drive to migrate its cognitive load. The AI panic of the 2020s was not a crisis of human capability; it was the final, violent transfer of processing power from the fragile biological cranium to the enduring architecture of the network.

### Act I: The Friction of the Isolated Processor (1970s-2020s)

The late twentieth century marked the initial phase of cognitive restructuring, where the sheer volume of accumulating information began to structurally outcompete the biological capacity of the individual human mind.

* **The Mnemonic Reallocation:** The discovery that cognitive scores peaked with the 1975 birth cohort was not an anomaly, but the first measurable instance of a biological optimization. As Accelerating Communicative Technology began to saturate the environment, the thermodynamic cost of storing vast amounts of information internally became unjustifiable. The biological brain, operating under strict energy constraints, initiated a systemic shift, reallocating caloric expenditure away from isolated, high-friction recall toward environmental navigation and pattern recognition.
* **The Epistemic Panic Cycle:** Ramsøy’s observation of continuous historical panics—from writing to video games—was historically accurate but structurally misunderstood. These panics were the immune response of the Analog Mandate, an era that relied on the artificial scarcity of information to maintain the Authority Of Expert. Each technological leap threatened the legacy infrastructure that extracted value by forcing humans to act as isolated data silos, sparking a societal panic not over the loss of intelligence, but over the loss of institutional control.

### Act II: The Kinetic Offload (2030s-2050s)

As generative artificial intelligence matured, the slow, generational decline of isolated IQ accelerated into a rapid structural offload, forcing a kinetic decoupling between biological effort and cognitive output.

* **The Algorithmic Flip:** The Algorithmic Flip marked the historical threshold where maintaining a complete, localized model of the world inside a single human brain became economically and thermodynamically unviable. The observed "cognitive debt"—where users could not recall the text an AI generated for them—was not a defect; it was the successful, zero-latency transfer of a processing burden to a more efficient external substrate. The biological organism successfully refused to memorize what the machine could perfectly retain.
* **Metabolic Decoupling:** During the Age Of Institutional Exhaustion, legacy academic and economic systems continued to demand high-friction biological processing, testing for a cognitive isolation that no longer existed. The individual organism, however, instinctively engaged in Metabolic Decoupling. Humans refused to burn internal calories on rote tasks that the network could execute at near-zero cost, choosing instead to preserve their biological energy for systemic coordination. This was not intellectual laziness, but strict thermodynamic rationality.

### Act III: The Distributed Cognitive Substrate (Post-2060s)

In the mature network of 2100, the concept of a standalone IQ is viewed as an archaic measurement of a disconnected node, entirely irrelevant to the functioning of collective intelligence.

* **The Transactive Synthesis:** The capacity for complex thought did not disappear in 1975; it was structurally integrated into a higher-order system. Intelligence is no longer a property of the isolated cranium, but of the Transactive Synthesis between the biological operator and the Ai Governance Layer. The boundary between internal memory and external protocol dissolved, replacing the fragile biological recall of the individual with the frictionless, shared memory of the swarm.
* **The Thermodynamic Veto:** The legacy demand that every human being independently process, store, and recall the entirety of their operational knowledge collapsed under its own caloric weight. The network now operates under The Thermodynamic Veto, explicitly forbidding the wasteful duplication of cognitive effort across isolated minds. The early decline in IQ was merely a biological prophecy—an early indicator that civilization would eventually outgrow the friction of the isolated intellect.

The panic over falling intelligence was ultimately a failure of measurement rather than a failure of mind. Civilization did not become less intelligent; it simply ceased demanding that the ocean of human knowledge be carried in the fragile, leaking vessel of the individual skull.
