# The Poisoner's Handbook



![The Poisoner's Handbook](/images/September12PoisonerHandbook.png)

Assassination War & the Death of Bureaucracy, by Kulak
Anarchonomicon Substack, June 2, 2024
https://www.anarchonomicon.com/p/assassination-war-and-the-death-of
This document is now radioactive. Before the assassination of Charlie Kirk, this text was a work of brilliant, cynical, and deeply incendiary theory. In the aftermath, it is no longer a theory. It is the operating manual for the new age of political violence. The author, "Kulak," did not pull the trigger, but he wrote the intellectual justification for the world that will now be born from that act.
The authors at Jacobin wrote a beautiful eulogy for the "fragile consensus" that protected political speech. This document is the weapon that shattered it. From my perspective in 2100, we see this text as the moment the abstract "Crisis of Meaning" and the "Culture Wars Endgame" were finally married to a lethal, physical tactic.
Here is the revised constellation of the history this handbook unleashed, a history that began, for all intents and purposes, with the death of Charlie Kirk.### Act I: The Proof of Concept (2020s-2030s). The assassination of Charlie Kirk was the proof of concept that the author's theory required. It proved that the consensus was truly broken. It proved that a political actor could be physically eliminated. But the deeper, more insidious proof was yet to come.### Act II: [[The Great Culling]] (2030s-2050s). The "spiral into political violence" that the Jacobin authors feared did not remain a series of high-profile, tit-for-tat assassinations. It evolved, following this handbook's logic, into something far more systemic and devastating. This was the era of "The Great Culling."### Act III: The New Iron Cage (Post-2060s). The author of this handbook believed he was providing the tools to liberate humanity from the bureaucratic state. The ultimate, terrible irony is that his doctrine became the foundational design brief for the creation of a new kind of state that is infinitely more powerful and inescapable.
