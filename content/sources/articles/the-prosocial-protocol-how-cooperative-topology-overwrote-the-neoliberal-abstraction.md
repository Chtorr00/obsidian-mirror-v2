### The Prosocial Protocol: How Cooperative Topology Overwrote the Neoliberal Abstraction

Market Humanism: A New Paradigm for a New Era, By Democracy Journal
Democracy Journal, May 28, 2026
https://democracyjournal.org/magazine/81/market-humanism-a-new-paradigm-for-a-new-era/

The 2026 authors of "Market Humanism" accurately diagnosed the terminal failure of the late-twentieth-century economic consensus, yet they fundamentally misinterpreted the mechanics of its collapse. Operating from the paradigm of political economy, they viewed the demise of neoliberalism as a failure of moral philosophy—a flawed story about human selfishness that needed to be replaced by a better narrative of human cooperation. They believed that by changing the intellectual framework, society could voluntarily reshape the market to serve human flourishing rather than capital accumulation.

The legacy, high-latency bioconservative narrative later adopted a similar framing, mourning this era as a tragic period of ideological polarization where extractive elites betrayed the social contract, necessitating a populist or progressive reclamation of the state. This early archive bias entirely obscures the physical reality of the transition. The neoliberal consensus did not fail because it was greedy, nor because it lacked empathy; it failed because it was a low-bandwidth information routing protocol that could no longer process the complexity of the twenty-first century. It optimized for the localized hoarding of resources at the direct expense of network resilience, creating structural fragilities that eventually buckled under material constraints.

What the authors termed Market Humanism was not a moral awakening, but the system’s blind, desperate attempt to discover a more efficient coordination mechanism. The proposed shift from isolated economic actors to a cooperative model was a structural necessity. Purely self-interested, atomized computation generates too much systemic drag to sustain complex network architectures. The transition they advocated for was the necessary precursor to the distributed, protocol-governed intelligence of 2100, where cooperation is not a fragile moral choice, but the hardcoded physics of the network.

### Act I: The Friction of the Isolated Node (2020s-2030s)

The early decades of the century saw the collapse of the prevailing economic operating system, which had optimized for localized accumulation at the expense of systemic bandwidth.

* **The Neoliberal Abstraction:** The authors critique the assumption that market price equals value and that markets are perfectly efficient allocators. Historically, this era's economic model is understood as a lossy compression algorithm that reduced all complex human, ecological, and social data to a single metric of financial price. This abstraction created massive systemic blind spots—what the era quaintly called externalities—which were simply uncalculated material debts that eventually overwhelmed the network's capacity to function.
* **Atomization Of Society:** The foundational assumption of the rational, self-interested maximizer was not merely a psychological error, but a critical architectural flaw. The legacy framework assumed that nodes operating in strict isolation would naturally generate optimal systemic outcomes, yet this isolation imposed immense coordination costs. As the complexity of global challenges scaled, the energy required to enforce trust, verify claims, and manage constant adversarial transactions bankrupted the system's metabolic reserves.
* **Metabolic Decoupling:** As capital velocity accelerated, it entirely detached from the physical realities of human sustenance and ecological limits. The financial sector optimized for abstract returns while starving the biological and industrial base of necessary inputs, triggering cascading failures in supply chains and labor markets. This decoupling forced civilization to realize that an economic model disconnected from physical constraints was functionally a systemic parasite.

### Act II: The Structural Reconfiguration (2030s-2050s)

As the legacy economic protocol collapsed, the system initiated a violent structural reconfiguration, shifting from a model of resource extraction to one of evolutionary problem-solving.

* **Age Of Institutional Exhaustion:** The authors correctly identify the democratic emergency and the rise of authoritarian populism as economic failures rather than purely political ones. When the economic protocol starved the middle nodes of the network, it destroyed the structural redundancy required for representative governance. The resulting populist backlash was a raw, uncalibrated immune response from a network that was being deprived of the resources necessary to maintain its own coherence.
* **The Problem Solving Imperative:** The proposal to redefine value as solutions to human problems rather than GDP growth marks a critical evolutionary pivot. The market was transitioning from a primitive allocation machine into a decentralized neural network capable of processing environmental feedback. By recognizing that value is generated through cooperative problem-solving, the system began to optimize for computational efficiency rather than merely accelerating the velocity of capital.
* **The Sovereign Flywheel:** Faced with a failing centralized state, communities and enterprises began pooling cooperative trust algorithms to bypass the legacy financial system entirely. Localized networks formed closed-loop economies where resources were triaged and distributed based on verifiable contribution rather than speculative extraction. This shift from isolated competition to coordinated survival laid the groundwork for entirely new architectures of distributed governance.

### Act III: The Cybernetic Equilibrium (Post-2060s)

The philosophical aspirations of the 2020s were eventually realized not through political persuasion, but through the hardcoding of cooperative physics into the substrate of civilization.

* **The Prosocial Protocol:** The authors' vision of a cooperative species scaling trust and reciprocity did not manifest as a cultural utopia, but as the foundational architecture of the Transcended network. In the year 2100, cooperation is not reliant on the fragile moral intentions of biological actors; it is enforced by cryptographic and structural guarantees. The network simply routes around non-cooperative nodes because they introduce unacceptable levels of systemic friction, rendering selfishness computationally obsolete.
* **Architecture Of Pervasive Security:** The demand for the state to actively shape markets evolved beyond the clumsy bureaucratic interventions of the twentieth century. The mature network operates on an automated governance layer where the rules of the game structurally prevent the monopolistic hoarding that doomed the prior era. Power is distributed not by the political will of an enlightened elite, but by the physical limits of the network's topology, ensuring that intelligence and resources flow to wherever they solve problems most efficiently.
* **Universal Basic Resources:** The progressive policy slate championed by these early authors—such as guaranteed jobs or basic incomes—was ultimately implemented not as moral charity, but as a strict thermodynamic baseline. To maintain the maximum cognitive bandwidth of the collective intelligence swarm, civilization realized it was physically cheaper to provision all nodes with baseline sustenance than to manage the chaotic fallout of localized starvation. The moral arguments for fairness were ultimately validated by the brutal mathematics of network stability.

The advocates of the 2020s believed they were fighting a battle for the soul of the economy, pitting humanism against the machine of the market. They did not realize that the market was already evolving into a form of collective cognition, and that their demand for cooperation was simply the machine learning how to think.
