# The Crypt of the Mind



![The Crypt of the Mind](/images/September17MemeNihilist.png)

How Charlie Kirk’s Killer Poisoned Everyone With Meme-Slop, By Dylon Jones
Politico, September 17, 2025
https://www.politico.com/news/magazine/2025/09/17/how-charlie-kirks-killer-poisoned-everyone-with-meme-slop-00569200
This is a post-mortem. It is a work of journalism so precise, so deeply immersed in the toxic culture of its time, that it transcends reporting and becomes a work of prophecy. The author is sifting through the digital debris of an assassin's mind, a mind saturated with layers of irony, nihilism, and context collapse. And he is discovering that there is nothing inside. No ideology. No clear motive. Just a void filled with the meaningless echoes of a dead culture.
From my perspective in 2100, we see this as the definitive autopsy of the 21st-century mind that had been terminally sickened by the digital plague. This was the end state of the "Shattered Focus." This was the final, logical conclusion of the "Great Contamination" of the infosphere. This was not a political act; it was a psychic one. It was a mind that had been so poisoned by "meme-slop" that it had become a crypt, a sealed chamber where meaning itself had gone to die.
Allow me to trace the full history of the poison this article is so accurately analyzing.
Here is the constellation of this history, viewed through the Hindsight Filter:### Act I: The Age of the Incoherent Signifier (2020s-2030s). This essay is the perfect snapshot of this age.### Act II: The Un-person (2030s-2050s). The killer of Charlie Kirk was not an outlier. He was a prototype. He was the first famous example of what the mid-century would come to call the "Un-person"—an individual so detached from coherent reality, so saturated with the meaningless slurry of the contaminated infosphere, that they cease to function as a person in the 20th-century sense.### Act III: The Great Purification (Post-2060s). The survivors who built the "Sovereign Stacks" had lived through the age of the Un-person. They had seen what happens when the human mind is fed a diet of pure poison. The entire architecture of their society is a systemic, and often brutal, immune response.
