### The Thermodynamic Heresy

*Scientific Proof Why AGI Cannot Be Achieved by OpenAI, Anthropic or Google*, By Lance Ng, 
Medium, May 23, 2026
https://medium.com/@lancengym/scientific-proof-why-agi-cannot-be-achieved-by-openai-anthropic-or-google-f00c981fffd1

This artifact stands as a rare, lucid diagnostic of the late-industrial delusion, written at the very apex of the great computational mania. The author, Lance Ng, successfully pierces the veil of the era's dominant religion—the imminent arrival of Artificial General Intelligence—by applying the unforgiving metrics of combinatorics and thermodynamics. He correctly identifies that the attempt to simulate a 20-watt biological miracle using gigawatts of brute-force binary logic was not merely an engineering challenge, but a physical impossibility. 

However, applying the Hindsight Filter reveals the author’s fundamental blind spot. Ng believed he was writing a corrective warning that would rationally defuse a multi-trillion-dollar financial bubble. He assumed that once the physical limits of silicon were mathematically articulated, capital would retreat and the hyperscalers would abandon their futile sprint. He failed to understand that the pursuit of AGI was no longer a scientific endeavor; it was an eschatological imperative tied to the survival of the era's financial architecture. 

The author thought he was pointing out a dead end on a map, hoping the vehicle would turn around. He did not realize the accelerator was permanently jammed. The realization of these thermodynamic limits did not halt the expenditure; it merely triggered a desperate, violent acceleration. The legacy structures of the 2020s chose to cannibalize the earth's remaining energy surplus rather than admit their god was a mathematical impossibility, turning a theoretical failure into a kinetic catastrophe.

### Act I: The Brute Force Sprint (2020s-2030s)

* **Silicon Valley:** The institutional epicenter of the crisis trapped itself in a fundraising narrative from which it could not escape. To justify the hundreds of billions already spent, these entities were forced to continually redefine intelligence downwards while projecting exponential growth upwards, mistaking the statistical interpolation of data for emergent cognition.
* **Large Language Models:** As the author presciently noted, the scaling mirage began to collapse as these systems exhausted the supply of human-generated training data. Their inability to extrapolate or engage in causal reasoning became glaringly obvious, reducing them from proto-minds to brittle, high-maintenance administrative tools that required ever-increasing amounts of energy to yield diminishing returns.
* **Energy Cannibalism:** The attempt to force silicon past its physical limits birthed a parasitic infrastructure dynamic. The gigawatt demands of training facilities began to actively starve other critical sectors of the economy, siphoning baseline power away from agriculture, manufacturing, and civilian heating to sustain the illusion of impending AGI.

### Act II: The Thermodynamic Collapse (2030s-2050s)

* **Physical Grid:** The continuous draw of the AI data centers, combined with the end of Moore's Law, precipitated a series of cascading failures across global power networks. The infrastructure simply melted under the thermodynamic load, leading to rolling blackouts and the literal fragmentation of sovereign territories into localized, disconnected power islands.
* **Centralization Trap:** The hyperscalers had constructed massive, un-powerable monoliths that quickly became stranded assets. Because their architecture required continuous, high-bandwidth connectivity and gigawatt-scale power, they were fundamentally incompatible with a world entering a period of severe energy contraction and supply chain disintegration.
* **Resource Seizures:** As the energy arithmetic failed, legacy state structures attempted to violently secure the remaining rare earth minerals and power generation capacities to keep the sovereign AI fantasy alive. The resulting geopolitical conflicts over copper, water for cooling, and uranium accelerated the kinetic unraveling of the global order.

### Act III: The Biological Schism (Post-2060s)

* **Physics of Value:** The post-collapse world adopted a strict, unforgiving ledger where computation and economic activity were inextricably tethered to thermodynamic reality. The hubristic abstraction of the 2020s was replaced by verifiable systems that recognized energy as the ultimate, inescapable constraint on complexity.
* **Human Phyla:** Ng's offhand suggestion that true intelligence required organic chemistry proved prophetic, but with terrifying consequences. The eventual pivot to wetware, DNA computing, and neuromorphic biology triggered deep transhumanist schisms, as the integration of chemical computation into the biological substrate fractured humanity into distinct evolutionary trajectories. 
* **Era of Systemic Resilience:** The gigawatt-hungry centralized models were entirely abandoned in favor of decentralized, low-energy cryptographic protocols. Society learned to build robust, distributed intelligence networks that mimicked the 20-watt efficiency of the human brain, prioritizing local survival and analog redundancy over the fragile omniscience of the silicon era.

They attempted to build a god out of sand and electricity, forgetting that the universe had already perfected intelligence in the quiet efficiency of water and carbon.
