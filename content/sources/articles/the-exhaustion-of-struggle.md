### The Exhaustion of Struggle

*Why China Treats ‘Lying Flat’ as a National Security Threat*, By Arden Chao, The Diplomat, May 1, 2026
https://archive.ph/cuoiQ

The author of this artifact provides a remarkably astute diagnosis of a terminal condition. Chao correctly identifies that the Chinese state's legitimacy in the 2020s was derived not from economic output, but from the propagation of a perpetual state of emotional mobilization—an ideology of "struggle." The act of "lying flat" (*tangping*), therefore, was not economic laziness but a form of psychic secession from this mandatory emotional state, which the regime rightly perceived as an existential threat. The author believed he was documenting a unique feature of Chinese political culture.

Through the Hindsight Filter, we see this phenomenon as a critical early indicator of the global trend of **Ideological Exhaustion**. The author’s blind spot was assuming this was a localized symptom. In reality, "lying flat" was the Eastern variant of a widespread silent rebellion against the core narratives of all 20th-century **Legacy Nation-States**. These states, regardless of ideology, demanded infinite growth, infinite work, and infinite loyalty from populations who were beginning to understand that the returns on their efforts had diminished to zero.
