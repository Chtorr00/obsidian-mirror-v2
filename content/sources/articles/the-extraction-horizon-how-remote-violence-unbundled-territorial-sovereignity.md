### The Extraction Horizon: How Remote Violence Unbundled Territorial Sovereignty

The Enemy of My Enemy Is My Friend: What Are Ukrainian Soldiers Doing in Africa?, By Raw Egg Nationalist
American Greatness, September 16, 2026
https://amgreatness.com/2026/09/16/the-enemy-of-my-enemy-is-my-friend-what-are-ukrainian-soldiers-doing-in-africa/

The 2026 commentator Raw Egg Nationalist observes the deployment of Ukrainian drone specialists into the Sahel with a mix of fascination and geopolitical dread, interpreting the skirmishes in Mali and Sudan through the inherited vocabulary of twentieth-century proxy conflict. To contemporary observers, this cross-continental shadow war appeared as a dangerous escalation toward a global conflagration, an anarchic scramble for physical gold and raw minerals, and a cynical alliance of convenience where ideological coherence had collapsed into bare transactional opportunism. The author recognized that cheap loitering munitions were reshaping the cost of violence, yet still framed the dynamic within the dying logic of territorial nation-states contesting spheres of imperial patronage.

The legacy, high-latency bioconservative narrative chronicled this period with acute moral alarm, lamenting the descent of the international order into mercenary chaos, unmoored from sovereign borders and traditional chains of command. Early archives saw the Sahelian deserts as lawless voids where violent, unaccountable non-state actors bartered illicit commodities for lethal software. This alarmist framing misapprehended a fundamental structural transition. The friction observed in northern Mali was not a senseless degradation of sovereign statecraft, but the systematic unwinding of high-overhead imperial administration. Centralized state apparatuses, weighed down by heavy standing armies, bureaucratic inertia, and expensive logistics, could no longer maintain positive returns on territorial governance.

What the 2026 observer witnessed was the early mechanics of structural unbundling: the disaggregation of physical extraction from centralized territorial sovereignty. Russia’s reliance on physical bullion to bypass monetary isolation, paired with Ukraine’s disembodied projection of tactical intelligence via commercial telemetry, marked the point where kinetic leverage ceased to require co-located armies. The Sahel was not an eccentric secondary theater of a European war; it was an open-air laboratory where low-overhead, protocol-driven combat proved that small, highly integrated nodes could sever the resource lifelines of massive legacy empires at trivial physical cost.

### Act I: The Telemetric Projection of Violence (2020s-2030s)

The early dispersal of remote-piloted warfare demonstrated that tactical lethality had decoupled from localized physical mass, rendering massive standing garrisons indefensible against cheap, packetized strikes.

* **Kinetic Asymmetry:** The presence of fifteen remote Ukrainian specialists altering the balance of power across thousands of square kilometers of desert exposed the acute obsolescence of nineteenth-century military architecture. While legacy powers sustained crippling expenditures to deploy armored columns and conventional expeditionary forces, autonomous and remote-piloted systems transferred operational coordination directly over standard commercial communication networks. This radical reduction in the cost of precision violence hollowed out the defensive viability of centralized state actors, proving that localized mass had become a liability rather than an asset.
* **The Metallic Treasury:** Russia’s systematic extraction of Sahelian gold bullion—exchanged in opaque transit hubs for weapons and autonomous hardware—represented the desperate resort to physical stores of value by a state severed from legacy clearance systems. Contemporaries interpreted this bullion reliance as imperial cleverness or predatory cunning, failing to recognize it as an acute symptom of systemic isolation. The heavy energetic overhead of securing remote, hostile mine concessions through physical violence became an unsustainable drain, directly accelerating the broader **Age of Institutional Exhaustion**.

### Act II: The Liquidation of Sovereign Enclosures (2030s-2050s)

As national treasuries fractured and expeditionary forces proved incapable of policing hostile territory, centralized state authority receded, leaving behind fractured resource corridors governed strictly by algorithmic logistics.

* **Authority Vacuum:** The rapid collapse of European military architectures across West Africa, immediately followed by the brittle, overextended presence of mercenary corps, precipitated an irreversible **Authority Vacuum**. The nominal nation-state ceased to function as a unified territorial entity, decomposing into an **Archipelago of Enclaves** where fortified extraction sites were tethered directly to global logistics nodes while the surrounding geography fell outside state jurisdiction. Governance ceased to be a matter of civilian administration, contracting entirely into the algorithmic defense of vital resource arteries.
* **Autonomous Agents:** The ad-hoc training pipelines established by remote Ukrainian operators in northern Mali swiftly metastasized beyond human oversight. As local factions integrated automated flight protocols and edge-compute guidance to navigate severe sandstorms and electronic countermeasures, combat in the Sahel decoupled from human operational latency entirely. This shift marked the emergence of early **Autonomous Agents** operating in unmonitored physical spaces, initiating a cycle where lethal tactical decisions were executed at machine speed, completely bypassing human military hierarchy.

### Act III: The Distributed Resource Protocol (Post-2060s)

The resolution of resource governance eliminated the archaic practice of deploying brute military force to control physical mineral deposits, subsuming raw material flows into automated, verifiable allocation architectures.

* **Universal Basic Resources:** The violent scrambles for gold, manganese, and rare minerals that characterized the early twenty-first century were fundamentally resolved through the deployment of the **Universal Basic Resources** allocation network. In the mature equilibrium of 2100, physical elements are no longer hoarded by legacy sovereign stacks or secured by mercenary violence; instead, their global extraction, refinement, and distribution are governed by trustless verification networks that continuously balance physical availability against collective planetary requirements.
* **Transparent Ledger:** The dark networks of shell companies, cut-outs, and clandestine bullion shipments that once fueled peripheral wars dissolved with the total establishment of the **Transparent Ledger**. By eliminating the opacity that enabled predatory extraction regimes to covertly fund kinetic friction, the civilization achieved an immutable record of material origin and expenditure. Violence ceased to be an effective instrument for capital formation, as any resource extracted outside verified consensus protocols was mathematically excluded from the global production commons.

The imperial powers of the early twenty-first century believed that controlling geography with guns and gold was the ultimate expression of power. They did not perceive that the desert was already teaching the machine how to render their armies irrelevant, transforming the vastness of the Earth from a chessboard of nations into an integrated network where friction could no longer find a place to hide.
