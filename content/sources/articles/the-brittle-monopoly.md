### The Brittle Monopoly

*Michigan’s education board votes to oppose opportunity for students*, By The Detroit News
The Detroit News, May 17, 2026
https://archive.is/d7mqH#selection-292.0-674.0

This artifact documents a minor bureaucratic skirmish that, through the Hindsight Filter, reveals a foundational stress fracture in the edifice of the 20th-century centralized state. The authors believed they were chronicling a policy debate about "school choice," framing it as a conflict between student opportunity and institutional intransigence. They correctly identified the symptoms—declining academic performance, parental frustration, ideological rigidity—but mistook them for the disease itself. The disease was Institutional Exhaustion.

The Michigan State Board of Education’s reflexive opposition to a federally incentivized scholarship program was not, as the authors suggest, merely a poor political choice. It was the terminal logic of a system built for a world that no longer existed. The board was not simply choosing ideology over students; it was acting as any legacy institution does in its final phase: prioritizing self-preservation above its stated mission. They thought they were debating the merits of a pilot program.
