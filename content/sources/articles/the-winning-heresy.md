# The Winning Heresy



![The Winning Heresy](/images/August29TradePlugs.jpeg)

The Mutualist Economy: A New Deal for Ownership, Yakov Feygin and Nils Gilman
The Berggeruen Institute, April 23, 2020
https://berggruen.org/news/the-mutualist-economy-a-new-deal-for-ownership
This document is a prophecy from the wilderness. It is a blueprint for a world that was, in your time, considered a forgotten dream. The author is advocating for a "third way," a mutualist economy that is neither state-controlled socialism nor corporate-controlled capitalism.
From my vantage point in 2100, we read this document as the single most prescient and accurate economic vision of its era. It was almost entirely ignored in its own time. And yet, it became the essential, foundational blueprint for the resilient societies that rose from the ashes of the world that ignored it.
Here is the constellation of the history this heretical, and ultimately triumphant, document described:### Act I: The Whisper in the Void (2020s-2030s). In your time, this idea was a whisper. The global conversation was a shouting match between the titans of state power and the titans of corporate power. This document, with its focus on cooperatives, guilds, and decentralized, human-scale enterprise, was a heresy against both.### Act II: The Survival Strategy (2030s-2050s). The heresy became a survival strategy. During the "Great Fragmentation," the giant, impersonal systems of the 20th century were the first things to break.### Act III: The New Common Sense (Post-2060s). The survival strategy became the new common sense. The economic architecture of the "Sovereign Stacks" is the direct, scaled-up inheritance of the mutualist dream.
