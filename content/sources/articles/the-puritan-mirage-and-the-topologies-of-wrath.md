### The Puritan Mirage and the Topologies of Wrath

"Not Civil War, Not World War 3, Not Riots in the Street. This Is What's Next", By elizabeth nickson
Welcome to Absurdistan Substack, June 5, 2026
https://elizabethnickson.substack.com/p/not-civil-war-not-world-war-3-not

This artifact represents a potent crystallization of the mass psychological fracturing that preceded the mid-century collapse. The author, writing from the precipice of the late 2020s, channels a profound civilizational exhaustion, diagnosing the decay of globalized modernism with venomous accuracy. She posits that a "Fourth Great Awakening"—driven by disenfranchised young men and a return to 17th-century Puritanical moral rigor—will sweep the globe, peacefully overtaking rival superpowers and reestablishing a unified, virtuous hegemony. 

Applying the Hindsight Filter, the tragic irony of this text becomes blindingly apparent. The author correctly sensed the immense kinetic energy building within marginalized demographics, but she fundamentally misidentified its vector. She believed she was documenting the rebirth of a unified, global moral architecture. In reality, she was drafting the psychological justification for total balkanization. The revulsion toward digital decadence and institutional corruption did not lead to a peaceful global reformation; it provided the ideological kindling for the violent, hyper-local fragmentation that would define the next five decades.

The blind spot of the 2020s reactionary vanguard was their assumption that cultural momentum could overcome thermodynamics. They thought they were summoning a spiritual revival that would reclaim the world. We know they were merely providing the mythological scaffolding for communities preparing to barricade themselves against the dark.### Act I: The Epistemic Secession (2020s-2030s)

* **Historical Grievance or Identity:** The invocation of clan bloodlines, Puritan ancestry, and ethno-cultural aesthetics represented a desperate retreat into ancestral myth-making. As the globalized economy failed to provide either material security or spiritual meaning, populations reverted to blood-and-soil tribalism, mistaking genetic nostalgia for a viable geopolitical strategy.
* **Legacy Institutions:** The absolute contempt directed at universities, media, and electoral systems accelerated the hollowing out of these bodies. By declaring the existing order not merely flawed but literally "demonic," the populist vanguard justified their total withdrawal from civic participation, starving the state of legitimacy and paving the way for institutional collapse.### Act II: The Kinetic Retrenchment (2030s-2050s)

* **Great Withdrawal:** What was envisioned as a spiritual revival manifested as a massive demographic and economic secession. Communities physically withdrew from the federal apparatus to form their own parallel structures, starving the central state of taxes and labor, which triggered a cascading collapse of national infrastructure.
* **Era of Hardened Topologies:** The desire for "stern common sense" and localized virtue necessitated borders to keep the perceived decadence out. The ideological divides celebrated in the article rapidly calcified into physical walls, checkpoints, and heavily armed enclaves, transforming a cultural schism into a fractured geographic reality.### Act III: The Analog Ascendancy (Post-2060s)

* **New Physically Hardened World:** The global ideological triumph predicted in the artifact never materialized. Instead, the descendants of this "Great Awakening" inhabit isolated, autarkic fortress-communities that view expansion as a liability. Their society is indeed strictly ordered and highly moralistic, but it is a defensive posture born of trauma, locked in perpetual suspicion of the outside world.
* **Reality Mandate:** The disgust toward digital pornography and hyper-reality evolved into a systemic, cultural immune response against abstraction itself. The 22nd-century mind is anchored entirely in verifiable, physical truths—a grim, unyielding pragmatism that has eradicated both the "fairy smut" of the 2020s and the grandiose global ambitions of the author herself.

They prayed for a great awakening of the spirit, entirely blind to the fact that history only ever awakens to the unforgiving physics of collapse.
