### The Schism of Control

*In turf battle over AI, U.S. spy agencies vie for more sway than Commerce*, by Cat Zakrzewski, Ellen Nakashima, and Nitasha Tiku
The Washington Post*, May 11, 2026. 
https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/05/11/trump-ai-regulation-commerce-intelligence/

This artifact documents a late-stage symptom of terminal state sclerosis. The authors believe they are reporting on a bureaucratic power struggle—a "turf battle" between the legacy economic and security arms of the American government over the regulation of artificial intelligence. They meticulously detail the arguments for control, framing it as a choice between pro-industry innovation and national security caution. This framing, while accurate to the participants' own delusions, is the central blind spot. Through the **Hindsight Filter**, we see not a debate over policy, but a panic response from a dying organism.

The authors were documenting the final, futile attempt of the 20th-century nation-state to assert its relevance over a technology that would invalidate its core premises. The conflict between the Commerce Department and the national security apparatus was not about how to steer AI, but which department would preside over the state's own dissolution.
