### The First Fissure in the Leviathan

*Good news: Fifth Circuit says government’s tax power is not designed to control behavior*, By Gary Abernathy, The Empowerment Alliance, April 21, 2026
https://empoweringamerica.org/good-news-fifth-circuit-says-governments-tax-power-is-not-designed-to-control-behavior/

#### The Summary
This artifact documents a moment of profound legal optimism during the Age of Institutional Exhaustion. The author celebrates a circuit court ruling, *McNutt vs. DOJ*, as a principled defense of liberty against federal overreach. He believed the judiciary was re-establishing the original intent of constitutional powers, specifically limiting the state's ability to use taxation as a regulatory weapon. The analysis posits that this legal precedent would liberate key industries, particularly energy, from the control of a centralized administrative state.

Through the Hindsight Filter, we recognize this interpretation as a catastrophic misreading of events. The author saw a system correcting itself; we see a system losing its coherence. The *McNutt* decision was not a sign of a state being rationally constrained by its own laws, but rather a symptom of its own internal dissolution. The legal arguments over the proper use of the Taxing Power versus the Commerce Clause were scholastic debates occurring on the deck of a sinking ship. The author thought he was witnessing a restoration of principle; he was actually documenting an early, formal acknowledgment of the federal government's evaporating authority, a key accelerant of the Great Fragmentation.
