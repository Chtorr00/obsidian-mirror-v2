# The Prophecy and Its Crooked Path



![The Prophecy and Its Crooked Path](/images/July21BitcoinNewSystem.jpeg)

What Bitcoin Did: The Beginning of a New Financial System with Mauricio Di Bartolomeo, by Peter McCormack
What Bitcoin Did, July 21, 2025
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iuFkMJyqKKc
Listening to this artifact again, with the full weight of history in mind, is a remarkable experience. The conversation is charged with the energy of a prophecy, a declaration that a new world is being born. In this, the speakers were more correct than they could have possibly imagined. But the path from their vision to the reality of my time was not the straight, triumphant march they foresaw. It was a journey through fire, exile, and an eventual, deeply ironic, vindication.
Here is the full constellation of the history this artifact set in motion:### Act I: The Ark of Exit (2025-2040s). The speakers were correct that their "new financial system" was the perfect antidote for the "Age of Institutional Exhaustion." As faith in governments and their currencies crumbled, Bitcoin became the essential tool of exit. It was a digital ark, allowing individuals and businesses to flee failing economic regimes and preserve wealth amidst the chaos of the "Involuntary Jubilee." Its primary function was not yet to be the system, but to provide an escape from the old one. It was the currency of the prudent and the paranoid, a bet against the world's established order.### Act III: The Unforeseen Vindication (Post-2068). The event that changed everything was the "AGI Contention of 2068." After a series of escalating incidents, it became terrifyingly clear that a true Artificial General Intelligence could, in theory, compromise any purely digital, centrally-controlled system. It could rewrite ledgers, create money, and manipulate data in ways that were undetectable. A profound realization swept through the world's leadership: in a reality with AGI, the only truly trustworthy system is one anchored in the brute-force, un-forgeable expenditure of physical energy. Bitcoin's "wasteful" Proof-of-Work, once its greatest liability, was suddenly understood to be its most essential feature. It was a "thermodynamic truth anchor."
