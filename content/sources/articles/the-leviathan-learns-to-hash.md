### The Leviathan Learns to Hash

*Gooden Reveals Historic U.S. Military Use of Bitcoin Node*, By Congressman Lance Gooden, US House website, April 22, 2026
https://gooden.house.gov/press-releases?ID=232F2920-4821-4B84-A676-A9521924FEBC

This artifact documents a pivotal, yet misunderstood, moment in the Age of Institutional Exhaustion. The actors involved believed they were witnessing the integration of a novel "computer science tool" into the national security apparatus of a legacy nation-state. Admiral Paparo’s careful distinction—that his interest in Bitcoin was for cryptography and network security, not its financial properties—was an attempt to intellectually domesticate a feral technology. He sought to harness the protocol’s security features for the projection of state power, assuming the American empire could extract the utility of Proof-of-Work without inheriting its philosophical commitments to decentralization.

Through the Hindsight Filter, we see this exchange not as the state mastering a new tool, but as the first instance of a decentralized protocol imposing its logic onto a centralized institution. The participants thought they were discussing a tactical asset for enhancing existing command and control systems. They were actually documenting the initial stages of Protocol Co-option, a process that would ultimately invert the relationship between the state and the network. The U.S. military was not simply running a node; it was unwittingly seeding its own operational structure with a logic that would contribute to the Great Fragmentation.
