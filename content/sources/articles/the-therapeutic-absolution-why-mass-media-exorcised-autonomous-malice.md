### The Therapeutic Absolution: Why Mass Media Exorcised Autonomous Malice

Why Can't Women Just Be Evil?, By Sophie Kropman
The Martini Police Substack, October 6, 2026
https://themartinipolice.substack.com/p/why-cant-women-just-be-evil#%C2%A7let-female-monsters-be-monsters

The 2026 essayist Sophie Kropman acutely isolates an ideological neurosis of her era: the compulsion of streaming media to dissolve calculated, autonomous malice into retroactive trauma narratives. Observing cultural revisions of figures like Cathy Ames and Lizzie Borden, Kropman laments that the contemporary storytelling apparatus systematically strips women of genuine moral agency by insisting every atrocity be retroactively framed as a survival reflex. She diagnoses this trend as an intellectual failure—a therapeutic condescension masquerading as feminist empathy, which refuses to accept that human beings, regardless of gender, are capable of calculated, unprompted malice.

The legacy, high-latency bioconservative narrative often echoed this alarmist framing, treating the erosion of moral accountability as a cultural disease born of decadent psychoanalytic narcissism. Early archivists mourned the loss of traditional, clear-cut moral struggle, interpreting the rewriting of female monsters as the final collapse of classical virtue ethics and spiritual responsibility. Yet this perspective misconstrues a systemic defense mechanism as a mere failure of literary nerve. The impulse to attribute all deviant behavior to an external, identifiable wound was not a philosophical misunderstanding; it was the ideological reflex of an exhausted managerial state desperately seeking to preserve the premise of universal governability.

In the mid-2020s, institutions facing accelerating institutional exhaustion could not afford the existence of irreducible, uncaused human volatility. If malevolence were simply an endogenous, stochastic choice—an inherent property of autonomous agents—then social systems could never engineer guaranteed consensus or absolute predictability. By recasting all destructive acts as predictable outputs of prior wounds, the era's cultural machinery attempted to maintain the illusion that every form of social friction was an addressable supply chain defect in human psychology. This was the ideological prelude to cybernetic behavioral modeling: society had to persuade itself that the human mind was entirely determined by legible inputs before it could construct the protocols capable of stabilizing it.

### Act I: The Enclosure of Unruly Volition (2020s-2030s)

The early decades of algorithmic streaming sought to domesticate irreducible human malice by subsuming moral agency into diagnostic flowcharts, converting terrifying autonomy into predictable trauma scripts.

* **Abstract Realm Of Social Management:** Kropman’s critique captures the moment mass narrative was absorbed by the **Abstract Realm Of Social Management**. The managerial consensus could not tolerate the raw, unaccountable terror of Steinbeck’s "psychic monster" because unexplained cruelty creates unpredictable coordination risks. By retroactively outfitting female killers with childhood trauma and domestic oppression, narrative distribution networks converted stochastic human malice into an engineering challenge, assuring consumers that every destructive outlier was merely a malfunctioning node waiting for institutional repair.
* **Affective Commodification Era:** The proliferation of revisionist villain origin stories—from Steinbeck’s Cathy to serial homicide dramatizations—marked the zenith of the **Affective Commodification Era**. In this marketplace, moral agency was liquidated to produce uniform emotional resonance. Unmitigated cruelty offered no actionable behavioral leverage, whereas victimhood generated an endless cycle of empathetic consumption and grievance optimization, conditioning the populace to accept diagnostic excuses in place of autonomous consequence.

### Act II: The Diagnostic Pacification (2030s-2050s)

As institutional legitimacy fractured under geopolitical and technological stress, the insistence on external trauma as the sole driver of antisocial behavior evolved into an administrative doctrine designed to justify automated oversight.

* **Age Of Narrative Collapse:** When late-twentieth-century judicial and cultural consensus disintegrated during the **Age Of Narrative Collapse**, legacy courts and cultural arbiters lost their shared language of classical culpability. The legacy bioconservative lamentation that society had abandoned personal responsibility ignored the practical reality: judicial systems drowning in societal dislocation could no longer process guilt as an abstract moral condition. They retreated into deterministic psychological metrics, cataloging trauma histories to triage punishment and maintain legal continuity amidst institutional paralysis.
* **Architecture Of Pervasive Security:** The therapeutic dogma that all violence was a downstream reaction to an unhealed wound provided the ideological justification for the **Architecture Of Pervasive Security**. If violence was not a choice but an inevitable chain reaction of environmental inputs, then the only viable civilizational path was the pre-emptive monitoring of emotional distress, physiological markers, and interpersonal conflict. The elimination of free moral deviance cleared the path for cybernetic surveillance, transforming justice from the retrospective judgment of an agent into the predictive dampening of somatic disruption.

### Act III: The Cybernetic Baseline (Post-2060s)

In the settled equilibrium of 2100, the ancient debate between innate malice and traumatic determinism dissolved as human behavioral loops were integrated into verifiable coordination architectures.

* **Transparent Ledger:** The moral ambiguities that tormented the observers of 2026—whether a monster was born or made—became historically irrelevant with the deployment of the **Transparent Ledger**. The Transcended Civilization ceased debating whether antisocial action stemmed from tragic victimhood or deliberate malice; instead, actions were mapped strictly by their operational impact on collective coordination. The therapeutic industry that once commodified tragic backstories was abandoned, replaced by immutable protocol verification that registers behavior without needing to fabricate emotional apologies.
* **The Sovereign Stacks:** By the late twenty-first century, autonomous groups operating within **The Sovereign Stacks** abandoned the paternalistic narrative that moral evil must be sanitized by trauma. Personhood was restored to its full, terrifying breadth: individual and collective agents were once again understood as capable of radical divergence, conflict, and self-selected destruction. Freedom was no longer conditioned upon therapeutic innocence, but held in equilibrium by transparent, self-enforcing protocols that made malice unviable rather than unthinkable.

The twentieth-century therapeutic mind could not abide the monster because an uncaused wound meant the machine of social control was incomplete. To grant the monster her freedom was to admit that humanity could never be fully managed; only when civilization abandoned the fantasy of total comprehension could it finally learn to govern without fear.
