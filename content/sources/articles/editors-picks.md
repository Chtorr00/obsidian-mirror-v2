### Editors' Picks

Silicon Valley Is Bracing for a Permanent Underclass
By Jasmine Sun
The New York Times, May 1, 2026
https://archive.is/yimb2

Through the **Hindsight Filter**, this article is a remarkable snapshot of the final, desperate moments of the pre-fragmentation economic order. Jasmine Sun captures the growing realization within the tech elite that their creation—ubiquitous, high-utility machine intelligence—was not just "disrupting" the labor market, but was effectively ending the social contract of the 20th century. The anxiety she describes was the psychological precursor to **Institutional Exhaustion**, as the leaders of the most powerful companies on earth realized they had built a world they could no longer govern or stabilize.
