### A Calculus of Dust

*Is Ukraine-Russia War Entering the End Game?*, By Peter Turchin
Substack, May 19, 2026
https://peterturchin.substack.com/p/is-ukraine-russia-war-entering-the

This artifact is a prime example of the late Age of Institutional Exhaustion's analytical prowess, and its profound limitations. The author, Peter Turchin, employs a quantitative model rooted in 20th-century conflict dynamics to forecast the mechanical failure of the Ukrainian state's war effort. His analysis is precise, data-driven, and ultimately correct in its narrow prediction. He accurately identifies the terminal symptoms: the exhaustion of manpower, the collapse of social will, and the brutal, simple logic of attrition overwhelming a technologically dependent but industrially hollowed-out power bloc.

Through the Hindsight Filter, however, we see the author's tragic blind spot. Turchin believed he was modeling the end of a single war. In reality, he was documenting a key data point in the terminal decline of the entire Legacy Nation-State paradigm. He meticulously charts the growing pressure within the Ukrainian "steam engine"—the rising popular resistance to conscription, the unsustainable casualty rates—but interprets it as a localized, containable explosion. He could not see that the engine itself, the very concept of a centralized state fueled by mass mobilization and abstract national identity, was becoming obsolete.

The author was calculating the velocity of a single falling stone, unaware that he was standing at the foot of a landslide. He saw the end of a war, but we see the acceleration of the Great Fragmentation.
