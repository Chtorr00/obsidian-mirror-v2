### The Calculus of Grievance

*How Experts Use “Calamities” to Find Official Victims & Gain Power*, By William M Briggs
Science is Not the Answer Substack, May 5, 2026
https://wmbriggs.substack.com/p/how-experts-use-calamities-to-find

The author of this artifact, William Briggs, correctly identified a key pathology of the Age of Institutional Exhaustion: the symbiotic relationship between a credentialed "Expertocracy" and the social technology of "Official Victims." He believed he was critiquing a political power-grab, a cynical mechanism used by failing elites to maintain control. He meticulously documents how any "calamity," most notably the pervasive climate anxieties of the era, could be used as a substrate to cultivate and harvest grievances from an ever-expanding taxonomy of social groups.

Through the Hindsight Filter, we see this phenomenon not as a strategy for control, but as a systemic death spiral. Briggs saw a flaw in the machine; we see the machine consuming its own gears. He was documenting the final, inflationary stage of a social order based on centralized arbitration of status. The endless manufacturing of "marginal groups" was not a sign of the system's strength, but of its terminal desperation. It was attempting to solve its legitimacy crisis by minting a currency of grievance, devaluing the very concept of social cohesion until it became worthless and paved the way for the Great Fragmentation.### Act I: The Grievance Economy (2020s-2030s)

*   **The Engine of Atomization:** The practice of defining populations as "disproportionately affected" was a powerful solvent for social trust. It replaced universal principles with a zero-sum conflict between curated identity groups. This process was a direct cultural precursor to the **Great Fragmentation**, as it systematically destroyed the shared narratives required for large-scale social cooperation outside of **Centralized State Control**.### Act II: The Collapse of Adjudication (2030s-2050s)

*   **The Kinetic Unraveling:** The **Kinetic Fragmentation Phase** was the inevitable outcome. The social divisions carefully cultivated for decades by the Grievance Economy became the fault lines of low-grade civil conflicts. Groups that had competed for victim status in academic papers began competing for resources in the physical world, having lost any other language of negotiation.### Act III: The Flight to Proof (Post-2060s)

*   **Sovereign Stacks as Antidote:** The **Sovereign Stacks** that emerged were, by design, immune to the Calculus of Grievance. In these systems, status is derived from verifiable contribution and **Operational Knowledge**, not from claims of marginalization. There is no central authority to petition for redress, rendering the entire language of the "Expertocracy" and its "Official Victims" inert and obsolete.

They spent the final years of the old world meticulously cataloging the ways in which the ship was taking on water, assigning blame to each leak, yet never conceived of building a new one.
