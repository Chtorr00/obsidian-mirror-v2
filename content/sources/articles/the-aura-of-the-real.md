### The Aura of the Real

*What will be scarce?*, By Alex Imas
Alex Imas Substack, April 13, 2026
https://aleximas.substack.com/p/what-will-be-scarce

Through the **Hindsight Filter**, we see this analysis as both profoundly insightful and tragically naive. Imas was not observing a healthy economic evolution; he was documenting a symptom of systemic decay. The desperate search for authenticity and human connection he described was not the luxury preference of a thriving society growing richer, but a coping mechanism for a society whose institutions were dissolving. He mistook the fever for the cure. The author thought he was charting the emergence of a "post-commodity economy," but he was actually recording the last coherent consumer demands before the **Great Fragmentation** rendered the global commodity economy irrelevant.
