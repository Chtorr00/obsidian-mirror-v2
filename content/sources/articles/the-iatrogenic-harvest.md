### The Iatrogenic Harvest
*The 12 Screenings That Manufacture the Patients They Claim to Find*, By Unbekoming
Unbekoming Substack, June 9, 2026
https://unbekoming.substack.com/p/the-12-screenings-that-manufacture

This document is a meticulous taxonomy of medicalized harvesting. The author identifies the mechanics of threshold manipulation, statistical sleight-of-hand, and systemic overdiagnosis, believing he is exposing a corrupt pharmaceutical-industrial complex driven by corporate greed. He meticulously tracks the arithmetic of harm, demonstrating how the medical apparatus invented pseudodiseases to justify a cascade of lucrative, mutilating interventions on asymptomatic populations. 

Through the Hindsight Filter, we see the author was documenting something far more profound than mere financial corruption: the terminal metabolic failure of the late-stage fiat system. The medical establishment was not simply making errors of caution; it was constructing a closed-loop economy of perpetual intervention. By redefining natural biological variation and aging as a "pre-disease" state requiring constant surveillance, the system attempted to offset its broader economic stagnation by strip-mining the biological resilience of its own citizens. It was the financialization of the human immune system.

The author's tragic blind spot was his belief that empirical evidence—autopsy data, all-cause mortality statistics, and logical deduction—could correct the trajectory of these institutions. He failed to realize that the legacy medical apparatus had decoupled from the concept of health entirely.
