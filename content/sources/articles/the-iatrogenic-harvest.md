### The Iatrogenic Harvest
*The 12 Screenings That Manufacture the Patients They Claim to Find*, By Unbekoming
Unbekoming Substack, June 9, 2026
https://unbekoming.substack.com/p/the-12-screenings-that-manufacture

This document is a meticulous taxonomy of medicalized harvesting. The author identifies the mechanics of threshold manipulation, statistical sleight-of-hand, and systemic overdiagnosis, believing he is exposing a corrupt pharmaceutical-industrial complex driven by corporate greed. He meticulously tracks the arithmetic of harm, demonstrating how the medical apparatus invented pseudodiseases to justify a cascade of lucrative, mutilating interventions on asymptomatic populations. 

Through the Hindsight Filter, we see the author was documenting something far more profound than mere financial corruption: the terminal metabolic failure of the late-stage fiat system. The medical establishment was not simply making errors of caution; it was constructing a closed-loop economy of perpetual intervention. By redefining natural biological variation and aging as a "pre-disease" state requiring constant surveillance, the system attempted to offset its broader economic stagnation by strip-mining the biological resilience of its own citizens. It was the financialization of the human immune system.

The author's tragic blind spot was his belief that empirical evidence—autopsy data, all-cause mortality statistics, and logical deduction—could correct the trajectory of these institutions. He failed to realize that the legacy medical apparatus had decoupled from the concept of health entirely. It had transitioned into an extractive mechanism that necessitated the very harm it claimed to prevent, incapable of dismantling its own architecture without acknowledging its role as the primary vector of modern morbidity.### Act I: The Pathology of Surveillance (2020s-2030s)

* **Watch And Report Mandate:** The proliferation of molecular, genetic, and radiological screenings served as the somatic precursor to broader state surveillance. The population was culturally conditioned to accept constant, invasive monitoring of their internal physiology as a moral imperative, normalizing the forfeiture of bodily autonomy to centralized bureaucratic authorities and algorithmic risk models.
* **Age Of Articulated Anxiety:** The psychological burden of constant false positives and manufactured diagnoses alienated citizens from their own physical forms. Individuals were trained to distrust their own asymptomatic reality, placing their faith instead in corporate threshold definitions that trapped them in a loop of hypochondria, rendering them deeply dependent on the very systems poisoning them.### Act II: The Iatrogenic Collapse (2030s-2050s)

* **Passive Refusal:** In response to the predatory nature of the diagnostic system, massive cohorts engaged in quiet non-compliance, simply declining to participate in the screening regimens. This silent boycott starved the biomedical-industrial complex of its raw material—asymptomatic bodies—accelerating the insolvency of hospitals and pharmaceutical networks that relied entirely on the conversion of the well into the sick.
* **Systemic Risk Offloading:** The collapse was exacerbated as the legacy state abandoned the victims of its own mandates. Millions who suffered from unnecessary surgeries, radiation-induced secondary cancers, and antimicrobial resistance from routine biopsies were left to manage their own chronic injuries, proving that the system's claims of "early detection" were merely a mechanism for privatizing profit while socializing biological devastation.### Act III: The Somatic Reclamation (Post-2060s)

* **Pragmatic Reformation:** The restructuring of healthcare stripped away the layers of speculative diagnostics and pharmaceutical profit motives. Medicine returned to a foundation of empirical symptomology and localized care, utilizing targeted tools only to track actual infectious outbreaks rather than manufacturing pseudodiseases through the infinite amplification of genetic fragments.
* **Unwatched Gate:** Today, the concept of preventive care exists exclusively within a framework where individuals possess absolute cryptographic sovereignty over their biological data. The era of mass, centralized diagnostic harvesting is viewed with the same historical horror as medieval bloodletting, replaced by a stable protocol where health is maintained through environmental harmony rather than clinical terror.

They believed they were mapping the frontiers of human longevity, blind to the reality that they were merely strip-mining the healthy to finance the illusion of control.
