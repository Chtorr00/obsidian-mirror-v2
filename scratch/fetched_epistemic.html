The Geometry of the Epistemic Divorce

By tracking the drop in Dirichlet Energy and the geometric alignment of relational structures across distinct categories, they thought they had demystified the machine's capacity for conceptual leaps. They mistook the formalization of non-biological reasoning for an interpretability milestone.

Researchers assumed that because they could measure the structural alignment of an embedding space, they could control the conclusions drawn from it. They were not building a bridge to human cognition; they were calculating the escape velocity of the machine mind.
